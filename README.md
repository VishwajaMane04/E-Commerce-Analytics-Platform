## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate data across multiple operational systems:
orders, customers, sellers, products, payments, shipping and marketing.

When these datasets remain as disconnected raw files, it becomes difficult
for business teams to answer questions such as:

- How are sales and order volumes changing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which sellers contribute the most to marketplace performance?
- How long does it take to deliver orders?
- Where are customers geographically concentrated?
- How do marketing activities relate to customer and order performance?

This project builds an end-to-end analytical data platform that consolidates
these datasets into a governed analytical layer and exposes business-ready
data through Power BI.
